# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People in Hyderabad seeking dermatology-led skin and hair care, typically evaluating a clinic before booking a first consultation.

## Product Purpose

Neo Skin Clinic presents its treatments, clinical approach, results gallery, location, and appointment path for visitors in Vanasthalipuram.

## Positioning

The clinic combines expert dermatology, FDA-approved technology, and personalised protocols for Indian skin concerns.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing Next.js site provides informational pages, treatment discovery, contact details, WhatsApp contact, and appointment booking. Keep factual treatment, price, doctor, and result claims already in the repository; do not add testimonials, certifications, or clinical outcomes without evidence.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Neo Skin Clinic. The user requested a beautiful, premium, luxury-spa presentation while retaining the clinic's medical clarity.

## Evidence on Hand

Treatment copy, clinic details, service catalogue, and remote treatment imagery are stored in the current codebase. No verified testimonials or proprietary photography were provided.

## Product Principles

- Make the consultation path obvious.
- Lead with calm clinical confidence, never hype.
- Keep information readable on mobile.
- Pair considered luxury with medically credible language.
